ABOUT DR. SANDESH

A clinical approach built around listening first.

Dr. Sandesh Mahesh completed his MBBS and MS at Mandya Institute of Medical Sciences and currently practises in Obstetrics & Gynaecology at Siddhartha Hospital, Tumakuru.

Women's health can involve questions that are difficult to explain, especially when symptoms change over time. The aim is to understand the concern, assess it carefully and explain what the next step means.
