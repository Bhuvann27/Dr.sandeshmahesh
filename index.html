AREAS OF CARE

Women's healthcare, without the medical maze.

A broad OBGYN practice covering the major stages and concerns of women's reproductive and gynaecological health.

Care throughout pregnancy, with assessment and follow-up tailored to the individual pregnancy.

High-risk pregnancies are assessed and managed according to the clinical situation and hospital resources.

Obstetric care before, during and after delivery, with decisions based on maternal and fetal wellbeing.

Includes menstrual concerns, pelvic symptoms and other gynaecological conditions that require clinical assessment.

Assessment and management depends on the patient's symptoms, history and individual reproductive goals.
